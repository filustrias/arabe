Prepositions and Place Words

A grammar chapter on the little words that place things in space — on, under, next to, in, from — and one tricky pair, لـ (le) versus ليه (leeh). Tap any example to reveal its franco and English.

Place words

Arabic - franco English
فوق - fo2 on / above
تحت - ta7t under / below
جنب - ganb next to / beside
قدام - 2oddam in front of
ورا - wara behind
بين - bein between
جوه - gowwa inside
بره - barra outside
على - 3ala on / onto
في - fi in / at

“X is [somewhere] Y”

Because there’s no verb “to be” (the Pronouns chapter), saying where something is takes no verb at all. Just line up [thing] + [place word] + [the place]:

الكتاب فوق الترابيزة el-ketab fo2 el-tarabeza the book is on the table
القلم تحت الكرسي el-2alam ta7t el-korsi the pen is under the chair
أنا جنب الباب ana ganb el-bab I’m next to the door
القطة جوه الصندوق el-2otta gowwa el-sondo2 the cat is inside the box
الكلب بره البيت el-kalb barra el-bet the dog is outside the house

في and من — “in / at” and “from”

These two carry most of your location work.

  • في (fi) = in / at: ana fi el-bet “I’m at home”, bashta8al fi maktab “I work in an office”.
  • من (men) = from: ana men masr “I’m from Egypt”.

A note worth keeping: with a verb of motion you often need no preposition at all for the destination — baroo7 el-sho8l is simply “I go (to) work”, with no word for “to”.

لـ and ليه — a pair to keep apart

They look almost the same but do completely different jobs:

  • لـ (le) = to / for / until — el-hedeyya le Mona “the gift is for Mona”, men el-sa3a talata le el-5amsa “from three to five”.
  • ليه (leeh) = why — a question word: betet3allim 3araby leeh? “why are you learning Arabic?”.

One is a little preposition glued to the next word; the other is a full question word that lands at the end of the sentence.

Examples

أنا من البرازيل ana men el-Brazil I’m from Brazil
بشتغل في مكتب bashta8al fi maktab I work in an office
الرسالة لكريم el-resala le Karim the letter is for Karim
بتتعلم عربي ليه؟ betet3allim 3araby leeh? why are you learning Arabic?
