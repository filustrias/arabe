Verbs of Movement 2: Directions and Places

In the first lesson you learned to go, come and return. Now you learn how to point the way — left, right, straight ahead — and how to place things in space: inside, outside, behind, in front. All of these still take the same بـ / هـ / ending system from lesson 1.

More movement verbs

A handful more, in their dictionary (he-past) and present (he-) forms.

Past (هو) - franco Present (هو) - franco English
مشي - meshi بيمشي - biyimshi to walk / to set off
سافر - safer بيسافر - biyisafer to travel
وصل - wesel بيوصل - biyiwsal to arrive
خرج - 5arag بيخرج - biyo5rog to go out
دخل - da5al بيدخل - biyed5ol to go in / enter
نزل - nezel بينزل - biyinzel to go down / get off
طلع - tele3 بيطلع - biyitla3 to go up / come up

Place complements

These say where something is. Most pair with a noun directly: 2oddam el-bet = “in front of the house”.

Arabic - franco English
جنب - ganb next to / beside
قدام - 2oddam in front of
ورا - wara behind
بين - ben between
جوه - guwwa inside
بره - barra outside
فوق - fo2 up / above / upstairs
تحت - ta7t down / below / downstairs
العربية قدام البيت el-3arabeyya 2oddam el-bet the car is in front of the house
المطعم جنب الجامع el-mat3am ganb el-game3 the restaurant is next to the mosque
إحنا فوق، إنت تحت e7na fo2, inta ta7t we’re upstairs, you’re downstairs

Dialogue: getting directions

Layla is on her way to Hassan’s place and calls for directions.

حسن — Hassan
إنتي فين دلوقتي؟ inti feen delwa2ti? Where are you now?
ليلى — Layla
أنا جاية، أنا قريب من بيتك ana gayya, ana 2orayyeb men betak I’m on my way, I’m near your place
حسن — Hassan
تمام! لفي يمين، البيت على إيدك الشمال tamam! leffi yemeen, el-bet 3ala eedek esh-shimal Great! Turn right, the house is on your left
ليلى — Layla
أشوف عمارة كبيرة قدامي ashoof 3emara kbeera 2oddami I see a big building in front of me
حسن — Hassan
أيوة، إحنا فوق، الدور التالت aywa, e7na fo2, ed-dor et-talet Yes, we’re upstairs, the third floor
ليلى — Layla
ماشي، أنا طالعة دلوقتي mashi, ana tal3a delwa2ti OK, I’m coming up now
حسن — Hassan
هتوصلي امتى؟ hatiwsali emta? When will you arrive?
ليلى — Layla
بعد شوية، خمس دقايق ba3d shuwayya, 5amas da2aye2 In a bit, five minutes

Exercise 3 — Reading comprehension

Answer from the dialogue above.

1. Which way does Layla turn?

Show answer Right — leffi yemeen; the house is then on her left.

2. Which floor is Hassan on?

Show answer The third floor — ed-dor et-talet.

3. How long until Layla arrives?

Show answer About five minutes — ba3d shuwayya, 5amas da2aye2.
