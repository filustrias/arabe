Speaking and Understanding

These are two of the first verbs you’ll ever need: to speak (اتكلم — itkallim) and to understand (فهم — fehem). “I speak a little Arabic” and “I don’t understand” come up on day one. Tap any sentence to reveal its franco and English.

How it works

The present and past you already know: بـ for the present (batkallim = “I speak”), endings for the past (itkallimt = “I spoke”). The future is just ها + the present stem without بـ: hatkallim (“I’ll speak”), hafham (“I’ll understand”).

Understanding likes the participle. Just like raye7 / gayy from the movement lessons, the verb فهم has an adjective-like form فاهم (fahim) that you use for the state of understanding right now. “I understand” is far more often أنا فاهم (ana fahim) than the full present bafham. Its negative, مش فاهم (mesh fahim) = “I don’t understand”, is one of the most useful phrases in the language.

Two ways to say “not”. With the participle, put مش in front: mesh fahim. With the verb itself, wrap it in ما…ش: mabatkallimsh = “I don’t speak”, mafhemtesh = “I didn’t understand”.

Examples

أنا بتكلم عربي شوية ana batkallim 3araby shwayya I speak a little Arabic
أنا فاهم ana fahim I understand / I get it
أنا مش فاهم ana mesh fahim I don’t understand
هي بتفهم عربي بس مبتتكلمش heyya bitifham 3araby bas mabtitkallimsh she understands Arabic but doesn’t speak

To speak — اتكلم — itkallim

Root: ك-ل-م (k-l-m) — reflexive verb. Future: ها + stem (ana hatkallim, huwwa hayitkallim).

Present Past
أنا باتكلم - ana batkallim أنا اتكلمت - ana itkallimt
إنت بتتكلم - inta bititkallim إنت اتكلمت - inta itkallimt
إنتِ بتتكلمي - inti bititkallimi إنتِ اتكلمتي - inti itkallimti
هو بيتكلم - huwwa biyitkallim هو اتكلم - huwwa itkallim
هي بتتكلم - hiyya bititkallim هي اتكلمت - hiyya itkallimet
إحنا بنتكلم - e7na binitkallim إحنا اتكلمنا - e7na itkallimna
إنتو بتتكلموا - intu bititkallimu إنتو اتكلمتوا - intu itkallimtu
هم بيتكلموا - humma biyitkallimu هم اتكلموا - humma itkallimu

To understand — فهم — fehem

Root: ف-ه-م (f-h-m) — regular verb. Future: ها + stem (ana hafham, huwwa hayifham).

Present Past
أنا بفهم - ana bafham أنا فهمت - ana fehemt
إنت بتفهم - inta bitifham إنت فهمت - inta fehemt
إنتِ بتفهمي - inti bitifhami إنتِ فهمتي - inti fehemti
هو بيفهم - huwwa biyifham هو فهم - huwwa fehem
هي بتفهم - hiyya bitifham هي فهمت - hiyya fehmet
إحنا بنفهم - e7na binifham إحنا فهمنا - e7na fehemna
إنتو بتفهموا - intu bitifhamu إنتو فهمتوا - intu fehemtu
هم بيفهموا - humma biyifhamu هم فهموا - humma fehmu

Participle of فهم: فاهم / فاهمة / فاهمين — fahim / fahma / fahmeen — “understanding / I get it” (m. / f. / pl.)

Useful phrases

بتتكلم إنجليزي؟ bititkallim engelizi? Do you speak English?
ممكن تتكلم بالراحة؟ momken titkallim bera7a? Can you speak slowly?
مش فاهم، ممكن تاني؟ mesh fahim, momken tani? I don’t understand — again, please?

Youssef chats with Nour about languages.

يوسف — Youssef
