This and That

This chapter teaches the words for pointing at things: this / that — ده (da), دي (di) and دول (dol) — and how to name the objects around you. Tap any sentence to reveal its franco and English.

How it works

One set covers both “this” and “that”. Egyptian doesn’t split them: ده (da) for a masculine thing, دي (di) for a feminine thing, دول (dol) for plural — used for near and far alike. A gesture or the word هناك (henak) “there” marks distance when you need it.

Two structures, and the contrast matters:

  • After a noun (the noun takes ال): الكتاب ده (el-ketab da) = “this book”. Here the demonstrative is just describing.
  • At the front, as the subject: ده كتاب (da ketab) = “this is a book”. Egyptian has no verb “to be”, so the bare demonstrative + noun already means “this is…”.

So el-ketab da (this book) and da ketab (this is a book) are different sentences — watch the word order.

Asking: ده إيه؟ (da eh?) / دي إيه؟ (di eh?) = “what’s this?”.

Whose is it — بتاع (betaa3). To say “mine / yours”, use بتاع, which agrees with the thing owned and then takes an ending: الكتاب ده بتاعي (el-ketab da bta3i) “this book is mine”; for a feminine thing, بتاعتي (bta3ti).

Using demonstratives in a sentence

For “I want / I see / it’s mine” you can use a couple of words that just agree in gender (the full verbs come in their own chapters). Negate them with مش (mesh).

English Masculine franco Feminine franco
wanting عايز 3ayez عايزة 3ayza
seeing شايف shayef شايفة shayfa

Negative forms with مش:

English Masculine franco Feminine franco
not wanting مش عايز mesh 3ayez مش عايزة mesh 3ayza
not seeing مش شايف mesh shayef مش شايفة mesh shayfa

Examples:

أنا مش عايز عربية، أنا عايز عجلة. ana mesh 3ayez 3arabiyya, ana 3ayez 3agala. I don’t want a car, I want a bicycle. (m.)
هي عايزة فستان، مش عايزة قميص. heyya 3ayza fustan, mesh 3ayza 2amis. She wants a dress, not a shirt.
أنا مش شايف الموبايل. ana mesh shayef el-mobail. I don’t see the phone. (m.)
إنتي شايفة القطة؟ لا، أنا مش شايفة القطة. enti shayfa el-2otta? la2, ana mesh shayfa el-2otta. Do you see the cat? No, I don’t see the cat. (f.)
عايز الكرسي ده

3ayez el-korsi da I want this chair

شايف الحصان هناك

shayef el-7osan henak I see that horse (over there)

الشنطة دي بتاعتي

el-shanta di bta3ti this bag is mine

مش عايز القميص ده

mesh 3ayez el-2amis da I don’t want this shirt

Dialogue: “What’s this?”

Sherif is looking around Nadia’s place.

شريف — Sherif

ده إيه؟

da eh? What’s this?

نادية — Nadia

ده موبايل

da mobile It’s a phone

شريف — Sherif

الموبايل ده بتاعك؟

el-mobile da bta3ek? Is this phone yours?

نادية — Nadia

لأ، ده مش بتاعي. ده بتاع أختي

la2, da mesh bta3i. da bta3 o5ti No, it’s not mine. It’s my sister’s

شريف — Sherif

والقطة دي؟

wel-2otta di? And this cat?

نادية — Nadia

أيوة، دي بتاعتي

aywa, di bta3ti Yes, she’s mine
