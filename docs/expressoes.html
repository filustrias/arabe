Useful Expressions and Phrases

How questions and the present tense work

Three patterns run through this whole chapter. Get these and most of the phrases below stop looking like random vocabulary.

  1. Question words go at the END. Egyptian Arabic usually puts the question word after the rest of the sentence: esmak eh? is literally “your-name what?”, and enta feen? is “you where?”. This is the normal, neutral word order — not an emphatic one.

  2. The present tense takes a بـ (be-) prefix. A plain present verb gets بـ in front to mean a habit or an ongoing action: beteshta8al = “you work / you’re working”, bas7a = “I wake up”. You will see this prefix on almost every verb in this lesson.

  3. There is no verb “to have”. Egyptian uses عند (3and) plus a personal ending: 3andak = “you have” (m.). The very same construction expresses age — 3andak kam sana? is literally “you have how many years?”.

Here are the core question words used below (إمتى is added for completeness):

Arabic - franco English
إيه - eh what
فين - feen where
منين - meneen from where
ليه - leeh why
كام - kam how much / how many
إزاي - ezzay how
مين - meen who
إمتى - emta when

Examples

A few phrases that show the three patterns in action. Notice the question word landing at the end, and عند doing the work of “to have”.

اسمك إيه؟ esmak eh? What’s your name? (lit. “your-name what?”)
إنت منين؟ enta meneen? Where are you from? (question word at the end)
عندك كام سنة؟ 3andak kam sana? How old are you? (lit. “you have how many years?”)
بتشتغل فين؟ beteshta8al feen? Where do you work? (بـ present + question word last)

Name

اسمك إيه؟ esmak eh? (m.) / esmek eh? (f.) What’s your name?
أنا اسمي… ana esmi… My name is…

Where you are / where you’re from

إنت فين؟ enta feen? (m.) / enti feen? (f.) Where are you?
أنا في… ana fi… I’m in…
إنت منين؟ enta meneen? (m.) / enti meneen? (f.) Where are you from?
أنا من… ana men… I’m from…

Age

عندك كام سنة؟ 3andak kam sana? (m.) / 3andek kam sana? (f.) How old are you? (lit. “you have how many years?”)
عندي… سنة 3andi… sana I’m … years old

Work and study

بتشتغل؟ beteshta8al? (m.) / beteshta8ali? (f.) Do you work?
بتشتغل إيه؟ beteshta8al eh? What do you do? (what’s your job?)
بتشتغل فين؟ beteshta8al feen? Where do you work?
بتدرس؟ betedris? Do you study?
بتدرسي إيه؟ betedresi eh? What do you study?
بتدرس فين؟ betedris feen? Where do you study?
بتشتغل ولا بتدرس؟ beteshta8al walla betedris? Do you work or study?

Languages

بتتكلم لغات إيه؟ betetkallim lo8at eh? What languages do you speak?
بتتعلم عربي ليه؟ betet3allim 3araby leeh? Why are you learning Arabic?
بتتعلم عربي فين؟ betet3allim 3araby feen? Where are you learning Arabic?
بتتكلم عربي مع مين؟ betetkallim 3araby ma3 meen? Who do you speak Arabic with?

Time and hours

الساعة كام؟ el sa3a kam? What time is it?
الساعة كام بالظبط؟ el sa3a kam belzabt? What time is it exactly?
الساعة كام في مصر؟ el sa3a kam fi masr? What time is it in Egypt?
بتصحى الساعة كام؟ betes7a el sa3a kam? What time do you wake up?
بتنامي الساعة كام؟ betnami el sa3a kam? What time do you go to sleep?
بتروح الشغل الساعة كام؟ betroo7 el sho8l el sa3a kam? What time do you go to work?

Understanding and asking for help

فاهم؟ fahim? (m.) / fahma? (f.) Do you understand?
أنا مش فاهم ana mesh fahim (m.) / ana mesh fahma (f.) I don’t understand
ممكن تقولي تاني؟ momken te2ooli tani? Can you say that again? (to a woman)
… يعني إيه؟ … ya3ni eh? What does … mean?

Talking about yourself — work

أنا بشتغل من البيت ana bashta8al men el beet I work from home
بشتغل في مستشفى bashta8al fi mostashfa I work in a hospital
ساعات بشتغل في المستشفى وساعات بشتغل من البيت sa3at bashta8al fi el mostashfa we sa3at bashta8al men el beet Sometimes I work at the hospital and sometimes from home
أنا بشتغل دكتور ana bashta8al doktor (m.) / doktora (f.) I work as a doctor
أنا باحث ana ba7es I’m a researcher

Talking about yourself — languages

بتكلم عربي شوية batkallim 3araby shwayya I speak a little Arabic
بتكلم برتغالي batkallim borto8ali I speak Portuguese
لسة بتعلم انجليزي lessa bat3allim engelizi I’m still learning English
بتكلم عربي نص نص batkallim 3araby nos nos I speak Arabic so-so
بتعلم عربي على النت bat3allim 3araby 3ala el net I learn Arabic online
بتكلم عربي مع مدرس من مصر batkallim 3araby ma3 modarres (m.) / modarresa (f.) men masr I speak Arabic with a teacher from Egypt

Talking about yourself — routine

بصحى الساعة خمسة على طول bas7a el sa3a 5amsa 3alatool I always wake up at five
أنا مش بصحى خالص قبل الساعة خمسة ana mesh bas7a 5ales 2abl el sa3a 5amsa I never wake up before five
بنام الساعة عشرة كل يوم banam el sa3a 3ashara kol yom I go to sleep at ten every day
بشتغل من الساعة تلاتة لـ الساعة خمسة bashta8al men el sa3a talata le el sa3a 5amsa I work from three to five

Other useful expressions

عندي فضول 3andi fodool I’m curious
عندي كورس ألماني 3andi course almani I have a German course
عندك شغل بكرة؟ 3andak sho8l bokra? Do you have work tomorrow?
نص نص nos nos So-so / half and half
العيش ده غالي el 3eesh da 8aali This bread is expensive
القهوة دي غالية el 2ahwa di 8alya This coffee is expensive

The last two show the demonstratives ده (da) for masculine and دي (di) for feminine — “this”. They come after the noun, and the noun gets the definite article: el 3eesh da = “this bread”.
