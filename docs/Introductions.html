Introductions

Everything you need to meet someone: ask their name, where they’re from, how old they are, whether they work or study. It reuses the verbs you already know (اشتغل، درس، اتكلم، اتعلم) — so here we ask the questions and let those chapters carry the conjugations. Tap any example to reveal its franco and English.

Two patterns that run through it all

  1. Question words go at the END. esmak eh? is literally “your-name what?”, inta feen? is “you where?”. That’s the normal order, not an emphatic one.
  2. There is no “to have”. Egyptian uses عند (3and) plus an ending — and the very same word does age: 3andak kam sana? is literally “you have how many years?”.

(The بـ on the present-tense verbs below is explained in the Present Tense chapter.)

Question words

Arabic - franco English
إيه - eh what
فين - feen where
منين - meneen from where
ليه - leeh why
كام - kam how much / how many
إزاي - ezzay how
مين - meen who
إمتى - emta when

عند — “to have” (and age)

Arabic - franco English
عندي - 3andi I have
عندك - 3andak (m.) / 3andek (f.) you have
عنده - 3ando / عندها - 3andaha he has / she has
عندنا - 3andina we have

Name, origin, age

اسمك إيه؟ esmak eh? (m.) / esmek eh? (f.) what’s your name?
أنا اسمي عادل ana esmi Adel my name is Adel
إنت منين؟ inta meneen? where are you from?
أنا من البرازيل ana men el-Brazil I’m from Brazil
عايش فين؟ 3aysh feen? where do you live? (عايش from the Everyday Verbs chapter)
عندك كام سنة؟ 3andak kam sana? how old are you? (“you have how many years?”)

Where you’re from — countries and nationalities

The nationality word doubles as the language name (3araby is both “Arab/Arabic”).

Country - franco Nationality / language - franco
مصر - masr مصري - masri (Egyptian / Arabic)
البرازيل - el-Brazil برازيلي - brazili (Brazilian)
البرتغال - el-Borto8al برتغالي - borto8ali (Portuguese)
ألمانيا - Almanya ألماني - almani (German)
أمريكا - Amrika أمريكاني - amrikani (American)
إنجلترا - Engeltera إنجليزي - ingilizi (English)

Countries, by the way, are feminine (the Pronouns chapter) — masr 7elwa “Egypt is beautiful”.

Work or study

These all reuse verbs from the Work and Studying and Learning chapters — here just as questions.

بتشتغل ولا بتدرس؟ bitishta8al walla bitidris? do you work or study?
بتشتغل إيه؟ bitishta8al eh? what do you do? (what’s your job?)
بتتكلم لغات إيه؟ bititkallim lo8at eh? what languages do you speak?
بتتعلم عربي ليه؟ bitit3allim 3araby leeh? why are you learning Arabic?

What’s this called? Whose is it? How much?

ده اسمه إيه؟ da esmo eh? what’s this called? (m.)
دي اسمها إيه؟ di esmaha eh? what’s this called? (f.)
ده بتاع مين؟ da bita3 meen? whose is this? (بتاع from This and That)
ده بكام؟ da bekam? how much is this? (price)

A few expressions to keep things going

Arabic - franco English
إيه الجديد؟ - eih el-gdid? what’s new?
مفيش جديد - mafiish gdid nothing new
بجد؟ - bigadd? really?
بس / لكن - bas / lakin but
عشان - 3ashan because / so that
عندي فضول - 3andi fodool I’m curious

Dialogue: meeting someone

Hany meets a newcomer at a language meetup.

هاني — Hany
أهلاً! إنت منين؟ ahlan! inta meneen? Hi! Where are you from?
Guest
أنا من البرازيل ana men el-Brazil I’m from Brazil
هاني — Hany
وعايش فين دلوقتي؟ we 3aysh feen delwa2ti? And where do you live now?
Guest
عايش في ريو 3aysh fi Rio I live in Rio
هاني — Hany
بتشتغل ولا بتدرس؟ bitishta8al walla bitidris? Do you work or study?
Guest
بشتغل، وبتعلم عربي bashta8al, we bat3allim 3araby I work, and I’m learning Arabic
هاني — Hany
بجد؟ بتتعلم عربي ليه؟ bigadd? bitit3allim 3araby leeh? Really? Why are you learning Arabic?
Guest
عشان عندي فضول 3ashan 3andi fodool Because I’m curious
